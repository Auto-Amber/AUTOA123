Why Meter the Refrigerant

The metering device sits at the inlet of the evaporator.
It controls how much refrigerant flows into the evaporator.
It drops high pressure liquid down to low pressure so the refrigerant can boil and absorb heat.
Slide 2 of 3

Thermostatic Expansion Valve (TXV)

A TXV senses temperature at the evaporator outlet using a sensing bulb.
When the outlet is warm the valve opens for more flow. When the outlet is cold it closes.
It uses a pintle, which is a ball and seat valve, and it is paired with a receiver drier.
Slide 3 of 3

Orifice Tube (CCOT)

An orifice tube is a fixed opening. Refrigerant expands as it passes through.
A low pressure switch cycles the compressor clutch to keep the evaporator from freezing.
When pressure drops below specification the switch opens the circuit. It is paired with an accumulator.
TXV pairs with a receiver drier. Orifice tube pairs with an accumulator.
Knowledge Check 1 of 3

A thermostatic expansion valve controls refrigerant flow based on what?

Answer: Temperature at the evaporator outlet. The sensing bulb reads the evaporator outlet temperature and adjusts the valve.
Knowledge Check 2 of 3

When the evaporator outlet is warm, the TXV:

Answer: Opens for more flow. A warm outlet tells the valve more refrigerant is needed, so it opens.
Knowledge Check 3 of 3

Which metering device is paired with an accumulator?

Answer: Orifice tube. Orifice tube systems use an accumulator. TXV systems use a receiver drier.
